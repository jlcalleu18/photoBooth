At SJ Photo Booth, we believe every wedding deserves more than photographs — it deserves moments that move. Founded by Jorge and Stephanie, we bring a personal, passionate touch to every event, crafting elegant photo booth experiences your guests will treasure for years.

We offer fully customizable experiences: luxury backdrops, curated props, instant printing, digital sharing, and personalized frames — all designed around your vision. Our bilingual team (English & Spanish) ensures seamless service from your first inquiry to the final print.
